# KWA FEE — Artifact 5: The Mule & dealers

## Problem Statement (from the user's perspective)

The shop runs out of beans mid-shift and the shift grinds to a halt — that is the party killer, not the exciting part. When the Grinder's hopper hits empty, one of us has to go out and get more, and that drive can go one of two ways: it is a boring chore that owns the shift's clock, or it is a pressure valve that makes the whole table laugh while the rest of the shop keeps spinning. "Who goes?" is a social moment and "who pays the dealer" is a running family grudge — both should be comedy, never spreadsheet. The dealer you pick should be a decision you can argue about at the table, and Ma Paddy shorting you should be a story your friends remember next shift.

## Solution (user perspective)

Once per shift, when the beans run dry (seeded to land ~60% through the shift), Big Vinny drafts the player with the fewest tips — "You ain't worked a lick, kehd. Mule time." Any other player can hit V and horn-steal the run, griefing the draft reads as redemption, not punishment. One short street drive in the SAME scene (no load, no scene swap): steer the van like a wet bar of soap, hold-to-charge a boost on the exact FLING charge curve, smash cones, buck through potholes, and either boost-leap over the pigeon or let it end you (tumble, beans scatter, ~3s, restart just past it, Vinny roast). At the fork, pick one of three dealers — Big Sal's (good, priced), Rosie's (reliable, boring), Ma Paddy's (pre-war, prices for YOU specifically, remember your grudges). Haggle by flinging ONE coin bag onto the dealer's scale using the exact FLING physics; the scale's target zone decides paid vs underpaid. Underpay Ma Paddy and she bursts out on a mobility scooter for a fixed 8-second scripted chase — she never catches you, the replay feed catches the moment for the CATASTROPHE REPLAY, and her price for you goes UP. Shop stays rendered and live the whole drive; the other players keep serving while the drafted player drives. The whole interlude costs ≤45s wall time, then the beans auto-load, the horn honks, Vinny says a line, and the shift resumes with the dealer's consequences already on the shop floor.

3. As a player mid-run, I want the trigger to fire once per shift at a seeded ~60% mark, so that the run pressures the shift without owning it.
4. As a player driving, I want to steer left-right with auto-forward speed and charge a boost on the FLING curve, so that the run reuses muscle memory I already have instead of teaching me a new control vocabulary.
5. As a player driving, I want the van to fishtail like soap and squash-land through potholes, so that losing control reads as comedy, not failure.
6. As a player driving, I want cones to pop and bounce with +laugh instead of hard-stopping me, so that the street is a playground, not a slalom course.
7. As a player driving, I want one scripted googly-eyed pigeon at the same spot every run, so that the group builds an inside joke across shifts instead of dodging random hazards.
8. As a player who hits the pigeon, I want the van to tumble, scatter beans, and restart just past it with a Vinny roast, so that the fail costs ~3 seconds and a laugh, never the shift.
9. As a player approaching the dealers, I want a three-way fork where Big Sal, Rosie, and Ma Paddy have different price/quality/variance deals, so that picking one is a social argument, not a spreadsheet read.
10. As a player who wants quality, I want Big Sal's honest scale and real tip cost to deliver fewer complaints and a small tip bump for the rest of the shift, so that paying up is a real investment in the same shift.
11. As a player who wants no drama, I want Rosie's same-as-always beans with no surprises, so that there is always a never-wrong-wrong choice.
12. As a player who wants comedy, I want Ma Paddy's pre-war beans to swing tips big and sometimes spawn a chaos event (beetle in a cup, stink, crowd-gasp), so that gambling with history is the fun pick.
13. As a player who haggles, I want the haggle to be exactly one coin-bag fling onto the scale, so that the flight comedy from the shop carries into the deal.

15. As a player who underpays Ma Paddy, I want her to chase the van on a mobility scooter for a fixed 8-second scripted beat, so that the consequence is a performance, not a pursuit sim.
16. As a player being chased, I want the chase to escalate with one more absurd gag per grudge tier (scarf catches a cone, she bumper-cars a hydrant), so that repeat underpaying gets funnier, not steeper.
17. As a player who shorted Ma Paddy, I want my price to go up on subsequent runs per a grudge multiplier that caps and decays when I pay full price, so that the grudge stays personal but never snowballs into unfun.
18. As a player buying beans, I want dealer memory to persist per cafe as a JSON contract, so that my history carries across shifts and later NPC personality work consumes it without rework.
19. As a player serving during a run, I want the shop to stay live so I keep serving, so that the run is pressure, not a shift-stopping cutscene.
20. As a Big Vinny, I want the narration (draft, pigeon, chase, underpay reveal) to come from the roast trigger table, so the voice stays deadpan Boston and never goes silent.
21. As the delivery axis, I want chase and pigeon-tumble moments to always become CATASTROPHE REPLAY candidates, so that a good van disaster is replayed and roasted like every other big moment.
22. As the /sim harness, I want every run scored against an explicit laugh-event list, so that "funny ≥70%" is a number, not an opinion.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Draft rule (D1).** Beans-dry once per shift: the machine/bean drain (Artifact 4) owns timing, seeded so the empty moment lands at ~60% of the 3-4 min shift. Exactly one run per shift; the meter drains on the seed, not live RNG. Draft target = player with fewest tips that shift, read from the Artifact 1 per-player TipLedger. [DECIDED in synthesis] Tip ties resolve by seed order, deterministic per seed. [DECIDED in synthesis] If the drafted player is mid-action (carrying a tray, mid-fling), the existing STEAL/verb resolution completes first; the draft interrupts at the next verb-completion boundary — the run never cancels player agency mid-physics.
- **Horn-steal (D2).** V key (existing binding) lets any other player steal the run at draft and takes the driver seat. One steal wins; contention resolves to the first successful V press. No vote UI, no schedule. If /sim later shows drafted players never recover tips (punishment reads), revisit: "random among bottom half" is the fallback, not in scope to build now.
- **Route & pacing (D3).** One street segment inside the SAME scene — no scene swap, no load, shop keeps rendering. Structure: ~20s active driving (cones, potholes, pigeon) → 3-way fork = dealer choice → ~10s dealer beat (incl. haggle). Total interlude ≤45s real time, ~30s active. Return is a hard cut: beans auto-load, horn, Vinny line, back in shop. Driver seat = camera swap only; van is a run prop, never a persistent vehicle.
- **Driving feel (D4).** Arcade: WASD/arrows steer left-right, auto-forward speed, HOLD to charge BOOST, release to lurch. Boost uses the Artifact 1 FLING charge curve verbatim — release timing is already in the player's hands. Van is a rounded bean (THE LOOK rule 5): wet-soap oversteer, potholes buck with squash-stretch, landings squash. Cones smashable — pop + bounce + laugh, never a hard stop. No per-frame allocation: van, cones, coin bag pooled; street/storefront static geometry merged into one mesh each (render-batched, ≤N added objects during drive, mirrors are fake — no extra view render). Perf regression = blocker.
- **The pigeon (D5).** ONE scripted pigeon on the shared stretch, same spot every run, googly eyes, felt wings, spring-bobble idle, deterministic per seed, never removed. Boost-leap over it, or it "ends you": tumble, beans scatter, ~3s delay, restart just past it, Vinny roast. Beans timer pauses during the tumble; the ~3s cap is enforced.
- **Haggle (D6).** Exactly one coin-bag fling using Artifact 1 FLING physics verbatim. Coin bag is a heavier throwable in the same rigidbody pool — zero new physics code. Scale has a target zone; bag weight + landing position decide paid vs underpaid. Paid reachable (sweet spot), underpaid reachable (light/short). One throw or nothing — no counter-offer rounds, no dialogue trees. If RATER flags staleness, add a wobble factor on the scale plate — never a new mechanic.
- **DealerMemory contract (D7).** Artifact 5 owns a minimal persisted per-cafe JSON: `dealerId → { perPlayerShortCount, perPlayerGrudgeLevel, tradeCount }`. Price function: `price = dealerBase(quality) * (1 + 0.25 * grudgeLevel)`. Chase trigger flag lives in the same store. This is the same single per-cafe JSON the NPC memory spec uses. Boundary: Artifact 5 ships store + math ONLY — no NPC personality, dialogue, utility pickers, or state machines. Artifact 6 (NPC cast) consumes this contract for dealers-as-living-NPCs and must not need rework. If a pre-existing refactor owns a Shift–Command seam, note it as an external dependency only; do not absorb its work here. [Memo references no such ticket; if one appears, record, don't absorb.]
- **Grudge model (D8).** Underpay increments the player's short count and, on Ma Paddy, rises grudge. Grudge caps at level 4 and decays 1 level per shift where the player buys from Ma Paddy at full price — converges, stays personal. Chase finds its escalating gag from the grudge tier (cap 3 escalating beats: scarf-cone, bumper-hydrant, etc.).
- **Ma Paddy chase (D9).** Scripted burst, not simulation: on underpay she bursts out on a mobility scooter, fixed 8s beat, van is always faster. Rear-view shot, shaking fist, scooter wobble, gaining on pothole dips, then she drops off-frame swearing. Chase events ALWAYS become CATASTROPHE REPLAY candidates (feed to Artifact 8).
- **Dealer choice (D10).** Three axes that never align on one winner: Big Sal — top quality, honest scale, highest price, good-beans bonus (fewer complaints, small tip bump this shift); Rosie — cheapest, dependable, boring, no surprises (the never-wrong-wrong choice); Ma Paddy — pre-war beans, seeded comedy outcome (big tip swing + fevered customers) OR chaos (beetle in a cup, customer flees, filth spike), and her displayed price is per-player: hidden grudge multiplier shown as "special for YOU". No dominant strategy.
- **Same-shift stakes (D11).** Dealer results hit mechanically and immediately: Sal +tip/−complaints, Rosie baseline, Ma Paddy tip swing ± and possibly an instant chaos event. All land in the SAME shift — choice carries consequences within reach of memory and feeds the roast pipeline.
- **Funny metric (D12).** /sim scores every run against an explicit laugh-event list: pigeon tumble fired, boost-leap over pigeon, cone-cascade (≥3 cones), Ma Paddy chase triggered, Vinny roast fired, tip-swing surprise (Ma Paddy outcome queued), beetle/chaos event, "special for YOU" price reveal happened. A run counts funny if ≥1 event fired. Gate: ≥70% of seeded runs funny. If the metric fails: cut the route to ~15s BEFORE adding any mechanics — shrink, never add.

## Testing Decisions

- **Good tests assert observable outcomes, not implementation.** A test that "the charge curve is reused" is worthless; a test that "boost release accelerates the van along the same power-vs-hold-time function as a cup fling" pins the contract that matters — the player's muscle memory transfer. Prior art: the Artifact 1 charge-curve tests and cone/rigidbody pool tests; reuse the same patterns rather than inventing new harness shape.
- **/sim is the workhorse.** Scripted seeded runs drive: one-interlude-per-shift (`/sim` asserts exactly one interlude per 3.5-min shift and no second trigger, tap at ≤20s, 60%, and 95% marks); interlude wall time ≤45s draft-to-back-in-shop; ≥70% of seeded runs fire ≥1 laugh event; shop does NOT stall (tips/serves still accruing during interlude, serve-capability maintained by remaining players). 20-shift sweep asserts each dealer picked <50% of shifts and per-player prices diverge for players with different grudge history — blocks >50% single-dealer dominance.
- **Kinematics tests.** Boost curve equivalence to FLING curve; cone smash never hard-stops the van; pigeon tumble ≤3s + restart just past; chase fires 100% on underpay and chase events are always replay-candidates (scripted underpay run, assert the replay feed).
- **Persistence/memory tests.** DealerMemory JSON round-trips across simulated shifts; price multiplier is exactly `1 + 0.25*grudge`; grudge caps at 4; full-price buy decays 1 level; short count increments only on underpay; memory survives a cafe reload.
- **Per-player math tests.** Same dealer, two players with different grudge histories, different displayed prices; paid (sweet spot) and underpaid (light/short) haggle outcomes both reachable with seeded throws.
- **Perf gate.** Drive scene (van + cones + pigeons + coin bag + static street) stays at 60fps at 4 players, zero per-frame allocation: pool counters flat across the interlude. Regression = blocker, per standing rule.
- **Haggle input equivalence test.** The same throw input that lands a cup lands the coin bag (same curve, different mass constant); a heavier-throwable registry entry exists for the bag — this is the contract proving "no new physics path".

## Out of Scope

- No free-roam city, no GPS/minimap, no traffic AI, no multiple routes. One street, one fork.
- No shop-visible-in-drive interaction: while driving you are a van, not a barista. Other players keep serving; the drafted player's station is covered by the remaining players.
- No haggle dialogue trees, no counter-offer rounds. One fling or nothing.
- No dealer buddy/friendship, no dealer romance, no dealer upgrade shops. Grudge only.
- No open pursuit sim; the chase is a fixed scripted beat, never a catch.
- No dealer minigames beyond the scale fling.
- Van is not a persistent vehicle between shifts — a run prop with a growing dent. No garage, no van upgrades. Pigeon-Powered Van Turbo stays a tabloid upgrade gag, game-state only if ever.
- No NPC personality or dealer dialogue beyond the single Vinny lines this run needs — dealer dialogue is Artifact 6 scope. Enforced by acceptance criterion.

## Refined Gate

Artifact 5 passes when ALL of the following hold, verified by /sim numbers and manifest-validated assets (no human-laugh re-test here — that belongs to Artifact 8):

1. Beans dry exactly once per shift, seeded ~60% through. `/sim` asserts one interlude per 3.5-min shift, plus no second trigger at ≤20s / 60% / 95% marks.
2. Interlude ≤45s wall time from draft to back-in-shop; ≥1 laugh event fires in ≥70% of seeded scripted runs. Blockers: drive-with-no-fun, interlude swallowing shift time, repeatable same-pick dominance.
3. Haggle is exactly one coin-bag fling on the Artifact 1 FLING charge curve; scale target zone decides paid vs underpaid; both outcomes reachable. Blockers: no haggle, no underpay path, new physics path.
4. Ma Paddy grudge persists per cafe per player: underpay increments per-player short count; price multiplier `1 + 0.25*grudge` applies to that player's price on subsequent runs; chase bursts 100% on underpay; chase + pigeon-tumble both produce replay candidates. Blockers: memory doesn't persist, chase not triggered, price not per-player.
5. Dealer choice non-obvious: /sim 20-shift sweep shows each dealer picked <50% of shifts and per-player prices diverge for players with different grudge history. Blockers: >50% single-dealer dominance, choice reducible to a dominant strategy.
6. During the run the shop keeps producing serves; /sim tracks tips/serves during the interlude and shows the shop does NOT stall. Blockers: run halts the whole shift.
7. No per-frame allocation during the drive scene; van/cones/coin bag pooled, static street geometry merged; 60fps at 4 players maintained. Performance regression = blocker, per standing rule.
8. All visible assets authored via the Blender bpy pipeline, manifest-validated (required-name list, ≤35,000-triangle cap, origins grounded y=0, per-submesh material indices). Blockers: any external/downloaded asset, any unvalidated mesh.
9. Boundary with Artifact 6 held: the shipped code contains no dealer personality, dialogue beyond run narration, or NPC state machines — those belong to the dealer-memory consumer.
